# Build your first resume with HackerLeap

Reviewed 19 October 2026. Written by the HackerLeap team about our own product.

A resume builder should make it easier to finish your resume. Start with the facts you want to communicate, then check how they read on a page.

## Start without an account

Open the [free resume builder](/resume-builder/). Add your contact details, summary, skills, work experience, projects and education. The live preview updates shortly after you stop typing. We currently offer one template. The public editor keeps the default font and sizes.

Public editing does not autosave. Keep the tab open, and use **Download my edits** to keep a JSON recovery copy on your device. That file contains personal information, so store it somewhere private. You can restore it through the recovery-file input.

## Check the exported document

Choose Preview PDF to inspect page breaks, or Download PDF to get the file. Rendering happens in a queue. The progress page opens separately, leaving your editor open. Anonymous exports expire after 30 minutes and are restricted to the browser session that created them. Usage limits may ask you to wait when the service is busy.

## Save versions for different roles

Choose Save to a free account. Sign up or log in in the new tab, then confirm Save private draft. The transfer expires after one hour. Your original editor remains open if you cancel or need to try again. Changes made after starting the transfer are not included in that snapshot.

Accounts can keep up to 20 private drafts and change fonts and sizes. The account editor autosaves. You can use a saved resume for job analysis, review proposed changes, and prepare for coding and system-design interviews.

## Review AI suggestions yourself

AI analysis is a separate account feature, not part of anonymous preview. Default allowances are 2 AI resume analyses and 1 AI mock interview per rolling 24 hours; availability depends on server configuration. Manual prompt workflows are also available. Do not accept invented experience, metrics or skills just because a suggestion sounds polished.
