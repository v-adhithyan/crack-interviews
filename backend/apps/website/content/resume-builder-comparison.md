# Choosing a resume builder: HackerLeap, FlowCV, Novorésumé and Resume.io

Reviewed 19 October 2026. This comparison is written by the HackerLeap team, so we have an interest in our own product. Competitor details below come from their official pages, not hands-on testing. Features and plans can change; check the linked sources before choosing or paying.

## HackerLeap

Our public builder offers one template, live preview and queued PDF exports without signup. Public editing does not autosave, and font controls require a free account. Anonymous exports expire after 30 minutes and have rate limits. Accounts support up to 20 private drafts, font choices, resume analysis, coding practice and system-design interview preparation. AI use is limited and depends on the configured provider.

The tradeoff is a smaller design choice. Choose it if you want a simple resume workflow alongside interview preparation. It does not promise an ATS score, an interview or unlimited anonymous downloads.

## FlowCV

FlowCV advertises one free saved resume, unlimited PDF downloads and more than 100 customizable templates. Its FAQ says you can start without signup, but an account is required to save, download or import. It is worth considering when template choice and visual customization matter. [Source: FlowCV product page](https://flowcv.com/).

## Novorésumé

Novorésumé lists a free Basic plan with one version, a one-page resume, predefined layouts and three fonts. Its Premium plan expands document length and customization and includes cover letters. Basic may suit a short resume, while longer documents require checking the paid feature set. [Source: official pricing](https://novoresume.com/page/pricing).

## Resume.io

Resume.io offers a free tier and paid download options, but its official pages are not fully consistent about free PDF access. The pricing page says free downloads are TXT-only; a help article describes a PDF exception for the Vancouver template. Check the exact export option in your account before spending time on a template. We have not independently verified that exception. [Pricing](https://resume.io/pricing) · [Free-plan help](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785088).

## Decide using the workflow you need

Try a small sample first. Check whether the required file format is available, whether the design fits your content, how progress is saved, and what happens when you stop paying or close the browser. If you need multiple role-specific versions, inspect the version limit. If AI is involved, review its data handling and verify every suggested claim.
