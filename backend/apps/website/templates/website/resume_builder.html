Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to download a PDF?

No. Anonymous PDFs are queued and available only to the browser session that requested them. They expire after 30 minutes. Rate and capacity limits apply.

Is my resume saved automatically?

Not in the public builder. Keep the editor tab open and download a recovery copy before leaving. Free accounts support private saved drafts and autosave.

Where does my resume text go?

Live preview sends it to HackerLeap for rendering without saving a draft. Export stores a temporary snapshot and PDF. Saving to an account temporarily stores a transfer for up to one hour. Expired artifacts are inaccessible immediately and removed by scheduled cleanup. Anonymous resume content is not sent to an AI provider.

Can I change fonts?

The public template uses Liberation Sans at 10.5 pt for body text, 12 pt headings and a 22 pt name. Font and size controls are available in the account editor.

Will this guarantee an interview or an ATS score?

No. A readable layout helps you present your experience, but hiring outcomes and parsing behavior vary. Always check your exported document.
