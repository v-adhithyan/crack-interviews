Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

HackerLeap is built to help software engineers improve resumes, match roles, and prepare for interviews. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using HackerLeap, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the service.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, resume content, job descriptions, account details, feedback, and support messages.

We may also collect technical information such as device type, browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to keep the service secure and reliable.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve HackerLeap, process early access requests, analyze resume and job description matches, personalize product suggestions, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, and communicate important service updates.

We do not sell your personal information.

Resume and Career Data

Resume content and job descriptions can include sensitive career information. We use this data only to provide the product features you request, improve product quality, troubleshoot issues, and maintain security.

You should avoid uploading information that you do not want processed by HackerLeap.

Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, email delivery, payments, security, and AI-powered processing. These providers are allowed to use information only as needed to provide services to HackerLeap.

Legal Basis and Indian Law

HackerLeap is operated as an India-focused startup. We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, where applicable.

We process information when you consent, when it is necessary to provide the service, when required by law, or when needed for legitimate business and security purposes.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and improve HackerLeap. You may request deletion of your account or certain personal information, subject to legal and operational limits.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and respond responsibly to security issues.

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. You may also opt out of non-essential communications. Some requests may require identity verification.

Children

HackerLeap is not intended for children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete it.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product and law evolve. If changes are material, we will take reasonable steps to notify users.
