Refund Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

HackerLeap is a lean side project and aims to keep charges minimal. Because access is digital and pricing is intentionally kept low, payments are generally non-refundable once access is provided.

No General Refunds

We do not provide refunds for change of mind, lack of usage, job application outcomes, interview outcomes, or dissatisfaction with generated suggestions after access has been activated.

Exceptions

If you believe you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or could not access a paid feature due to a verified technical issue caused by HackerLeap, contact us with payment details. We may review and decide an exception at our discretion.

Beta Access

Some HackerLeap features may be beta, experimental, or invite-only. Beta features may change, break, or be removed as the product evolves.
